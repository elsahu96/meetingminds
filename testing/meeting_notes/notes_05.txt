Meeting 5 — Customer Success Sync
Date & Time: Thursday, February 6, 2026 | 11:00 AM
Attendees: Rachel Holm (CS Manager), Jake Novak (Account Executive), Ines Carvalho (Customer Onboarding), Derek Yuen (Support Lead)
Transcript


Rachel Holm: Let's get into it. I pulled the escalation data before this meeting and we're up twenty-two percent in ticket volume week-over-week. That's not a blip — that's a trend. I want to understand the root causes and make sure we're routing them to the right internal teams. Derek, give us the breakdown.
Derek Yuen: Ticket volume is up across three categories. The biggest driver — about forty percent of the new volume — is confusion around notification settings. Customers are reporting two distinct problems. First, they can't locate the notification preferences in the portal at all — the navigation is non-obvious. Second, those who do find it say the settings don't stick — they update their preferences and then notifications continue to behave as before. It looks like the preferences UI and the backend are out of sync.
Rachel Holm: That's a product defect, not a user error. Have we filed a bug?
Derek Yuen: I filed a formal bug report last Tuesday with a full reproduction script. As of this morning, no response and no status update. I'm not even sure which team owns notifications on the product side.
Jake Novak: I can tell you it's a serious commercial issue. I had QBRs with three enterprise accounts this week — Meridian, Axon Group, and Clearfield — and all three raised the notification problem unprompted. Meridian specifically said their team had 'given up' trying to configure it and was considering reverting to email-only communication with customers. That is a very bad signal for a product that's supposed to drive engagement.
Rachel Holm: Jake, what did you tell them?
Jake Novak: I told them it was known and being addressed. Which felt dishonest because as far as I know, it isn't being addressed — at least not by anyone who's told us about it.
Ines Carvalho: From onboarding, I'm seeing the same issue but at the very beginning of the customer journey. Rachel Holm: How many new customers hit that step per week?
Ines Carvalho: About forty. So on a bad week, I'm spending two to three hours on manual workarounds for a product problem. That's time I should be spending on proactive onboarding.
Derek Yuen: I've heard through the grapevine — genuinely not confirmed — that the Engineering team has a workstream underway on the notification service. But I don't know if that's a new build, a fix, or just scoping. And nobody's been in touch with Support to tell us what to communicate to customers in the meantime.

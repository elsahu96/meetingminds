Meeting 4 — Marketing Campaign Kickoff

Transcript


Sophia Grant: Welcome everyone. This is the formal kickoff for our Q2 brand awareness campaign. Leo, you're up first. Walk us through the content plan.

The quality is good — I had one reviewed externally and the feedback was strong.

Sophia Grant: Ten days is too long. What's the status — is it sitting in a queue, or is someone actively reviewing it?
Leo Marsh: I genuinely don't know. I submitted through the standard Legal portal and got an auto-confirmation, but nothing since.
Fatima Hussain: I'm in the same boat. The ad copy for the LinkedIn creative — four different variants — has been sitting in Legal review for a week. I can't go to the ad studio to produce creatives until copy is approved. So the entire paid channel is blocked right now.

UTM structures, pixel configuration, dashboard builds in our BI tool. I can start the framework now, but I need confirmed URLs and final copy to finish the tagging. If Legal review slips by another two weeks, we'll be scrambling to get tracking live before launch.

Fatima Hussain: Not really. The slide was brief and didn't list an owner. It might be a Growth initiative or a Product launch thing. But if they're running paid, someone in this building has a budget approved for it.
Leo Marsh: It would also affect editorial. If there's another content push happening simultaneously around notifications, our thought leadership on customer experience might get visually or editorially confused with a product-specific campaign. We'd want to at least coordinate the messaging hierarchy.
Sophia Grant: I'll track it down. I'll ask in the marketing Slack channel today and if that doesn't surface the owner, I'll go to leadership directly. We can't have uncoordinated campaigns in market at the same time.
Owen Bradley: One more thing — I want to raise the attribution model for this campaign. Brand awareness is inherently difficult to attribute to revenue. If we're asked to justify campaign ROI at the end of Q2, we need to agree now on how we're measuring it. I'd suggest a combination of brand search volume lift, share of voice metrics, and influenced pipeline — but leadership needs to sign off on that model before we start, not after.
Sophia Grant: That's the right instinct, Owen. Put together a one-pager on the proposed measurement framework and I'll get it in front of the CMO this week. I'd rather have that conversation now than justify the spend retroactively.
